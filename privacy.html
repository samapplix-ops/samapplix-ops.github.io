Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

SamApplix ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications handle information, including apps directed at or suitable for children.

1. No Personal Data Collection

SamApplix apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data that could be used to identify you. Personal data includes names, account names, user names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or any other information that could be used to reveal your identity. The one thing an app may keep is described under Microphone below: recordings made by a grown-up, which stay on the device and never reach us.

2. Permissions

Our apps are designed to limit requested permissions to only those necessary for providing you with the best possible app experience. Any permissions used are specific to the needs of each individual app and are clearly identified on that app's page in the Google Play Store under the "Permissions" and "Data safety" tabs.

3. Microphone

FirstWords can record a grown-up - a parent, a grandparent - saying the words, so that a child hears them in a familiar voice. Recording can be started only after answering the question for grown-ups and allowing the microphone. The recordings, and any word typed with them, are kept inside the app on the device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them in the app at any time; clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them from the device. If Android backup is turned on, Android includes them in the device's backup to your own Google account, as it does with the rest of the app's data; we have no access to that backup.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This application is designed to be suitable for children. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy, we do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal information from children or from any user. The app does not require account creation, login, or the entry of any personal details to function.

We do not serve behaviorally targeted advertising to children, and we do not use any third-party services within the app that would collect personal data from children.

5. Data Sharing

Since no personal data is collected, none is shared with, sold to, or transferred to third parties.

6. Data Security

Because our apps do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information at risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be changed at our discretion. Any changes will be posted openly and visibly on this page. By downloading or installing our apps, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and discontinue use of our applications.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: samapplix@gmail.com
